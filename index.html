Sleep2Word

Peripheral-to-EEG Translation via Duration-Aware Wordization

Learning variable-duration physiological words to translate respiratory, ECG and PPG recordings into EEG spectrograms. The work explores compact cross-modal representations and block-autoregressive translation on large-scale sleep data.

Keondo Park, Arthur Delhaye, Younghoon Na, Hyun-Woo Shin, Hyung-Sin Kim
